--- F. monotonicity: is the line a deterministic function of the state? ---
Pirates with the same strength and the same weight offset are identical
inputs to the game.  Inside one arena, if such a pirate has at least as many
favourites, no more allergies and a later (better) position than its twin,
its win probability cannot be lower, so a deterministic odds maker must not
publish worse odds for it.  This needs no model of the game.

The histogram is only reproduced when the probability sits on a 1% grid,
which for a monte carlo means N=100; but at N=100 the line would invert
dominance far more often than it does, and a fixed rule already beats the
N=100 predictability ceiling.  Larger N fixes the noise and breaks the
histogram.  No single N does both.

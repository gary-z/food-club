--- 3. what each bucket actually pays ---
EV of a 1-unit bet = odds * win rate.  Under the finding-41 rule the odds
maker's probability is rounded to whole percent BEFORE 1/p is floored, so
the top of each percent tile can pay over 1 even when the odds maker is
exactly right: pct=9 -> odds 11 pays 11*p for p up to 9.5%, i.e. 1.045.

--- 4. which arenas the line helps on ---

effect except telling apart the twin pairs of finding 46, and it cannot
express a per-era effect such as Orvinn's pre-PHP-fix allergy damage
(findings 4c/34).  If the line's extra signal sits in those places it is a
gap in our features, not superior knowledge on the odds maker's part.

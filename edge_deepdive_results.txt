------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Counting the tests run across all three scripts: 11 odds bins, 160
  (pirate x odds) cells, 210 (odds x context) cells, 340 (pirate x food)
  cells, 11 EV thresholds, 57 (odds,T) cells, 3 NN variants.
  A z of 2.75 on one of ~800 correlated cells is unremarkable; the
  boundary-null bootstrap already returned FWER p=0.03 for the threshold
  family alone, and p=0.07 for the (odds,T) family. Neither survives
  the full search, which is why replication above is the deciding test.
